The CLI barrier

Why skills.sh shouldn’t need a terminal to use.

The best prompt systems on skills.sh were written for marketers, copywriters, designers and operators. The distribution method was not. If the only way to open a skill is npx skills add, most of the people who would use it never will.

Who this is for

Built for people who ship work, not people who debug npm.

01

Marketers & growth leads

Open A/B testing, ads and launch playbooks without installing a developer runtime just to read a prompt.

02

Copywriters & content teams

Copy a landing-page or cold-email skill into ChatGPT or Claude in one click instead of cloning a GitHub tree.

03

Designers

Use SVG, brand and visual skills in the browser. No Node.js, no path errors, no buried SKILL.md files.

04

Developers who want a GUI

Paste any repo or npx command into the compiler when you still want the skill, just not the CLI ceremony.
