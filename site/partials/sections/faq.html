All questions
What exactly is skills.sh?

skills.sh is a modern standard for packaging modular AI instructions, prompts, and playbooks. Instead of asking an AI generic questions, a "skill" gives it deep domain knowledge—like expert marketing tactics, copywriting formulas, or code architectures.

Why did you build this Extractor?

skills.sh was originally designed for developers using the command line with npm. But most people who need copywriting, marketing, design, and growth skills aren't developers. We built this tool so anyone can browse, unpack, and copy any skill in 1-click right from their web browser.

Do I need to install Node.js, Git, or anything else?

No. Zero installation. Everything runs 100% inside your browser using WebAssembly. You can use it on any Mac, Windows, Linux, Chromebook, or mobile device.

How do I use an extracted skill with ChatGPT or Claude?

Simply click "Copy Prompt". Then open ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI chat window, paste the prompt as your first message (or add it to your Custom Instructions / Projects), and start chatting!

Is my data private?

Yes, 100%. Because the extractor runs entirely client-side with WebAssembly and local IndexedDB, your files, prompts, and repositories are processed directly in your browser. Nothing is ever saved to an external database.
